## Overview

**Metadata Manager** is a centralized module used to define, organize, govern, and manage business and technical metadata across an enterprise data platform. It acts as a **business glossary and data catalog repository**, helping users understand what data exists, where it resides, how it is classified, and how it should be used.

In simple terms, Metadata Manager answers questions such as:

* What data assets are available?
* What does a particular field or dataset mean?
* Who owns the data?
* How is the data classified?
* What business terms and definitions are associated with the data?
* How are datasets related to business processes and domains?

It improves **data discovery, governance, consistency, compliance, and collaboration** between business and technical users..
