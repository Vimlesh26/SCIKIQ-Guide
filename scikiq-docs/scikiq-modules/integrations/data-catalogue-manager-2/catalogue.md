***

***

### Overview

Data Catalogs provide a centralized repository for discovering, organizing, and managing enterprise data assets. They help users quickly locate datasets, understand their business context, and determine their suitability for analysis.

Data Catalogs also improve data visibility, reduce data sprawl, and provide a clear view of data relationships across the enterprise.

These capabilities enable data stewards, analysts, engineers, and business users to efficiently discover, manage, and utilize enterprise data, helping organizations maximize the value of their data assets.

***

### **Manage Catalogues**

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Add a Catalogue" %}
**Overview**

The purpose of **adding a catalogue** is to register and organize data assets in a centralized repository so that users can easily discover, understand, and access relevant data. A catalogue serves as a structured inventory of datasets, making data management and governance more efficient across the organization.

***

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to add a catalogue:

1. On the **SCIKIQ Left Navigation Bar**, expand **Data Catalogue Manager**, and then click **Catalogue.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2100).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Catalogue** page is displayed.

2. On the **Catalogue** page, click the **Add Catalogue** button on the top right corner.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2101).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Add Catalogue** dialogue box is displayed.

3. On the **Add Catalogue** dialogue box, specify **Catalogue Name, Catalogue Description, Entity, Domain, Catalogue Owner, Delegate**, and then click the **Save** button.

> <mark style="color:red;">**Note:**</mark> **Delegate** is the user who is authorized to act on behalf of the catalogue owner or steward.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2102).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% tab title="Update a Catalogue" %}
**Overview**

A catalogue should be updated whenever any significant change occurs that impacts the accuracy, usability, governance, or discoverability of the data asset it represents.&#x20;

Updating a catalogue helps maintain a trustworthy and up-to-date inventory of data assets, enabling users to discover, understand, and use data effectively.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to update an existing catalogue:

1. On the **Catalogues** page, click the **View Catalogue Info** button corresponding to the required catalogue.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2117).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Edit** **Catalogue** page is displayed.

2. On the **Edit** **Catalogue** page, make the required changes, and then click the **Save** button at the bottom.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2118).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The catalogue is updated.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Delete a Catalogue" %}
**Purpose**

A catalogue should be **deleted when it is no longer needed, relevant, or valid within the organization's data ecosystem**. Removing obsolete catalogues helps maintain an accurate and clutter-free data inventory.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to delete an existing catalogue:

1. On the **Catalogues** page, click the **Delete** button corresponding to the required catalogue.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2122).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Catalogue Delete** message box is displayed.

2. On the **Catalogue Delete** message box, review the message, and then click the **Yes** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2123).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The catalogue is deleted.
{% endtab %}
