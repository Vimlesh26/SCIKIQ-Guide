## Overview

**What are Domains and Subdomains?**

A **Domain** is a high-level business area used to group related data assets. It helps organize information by subject, making data easier to manage, govern, and discover. Examples of domains include Customer, Product, Employee, Finance, and Sales.

A **Subdomain** is a specialized category within a domain that provides greater detail and structure. It helps break down large data areas into smaller, more manageable sections. For example, the Customer domain may contain subdomains such as Personal Information, Contact Details, and Purchase History.

**Purpose of Domains and Subdomains**

Domains and subdomains create a logical framework for organizing enterprise data. They improve data discovery, simplify governance, strengthen access control, and enhance scalability as data volumes grow. This structured approach also helps users quickly locate and understand relevant information.

{% tab title="Add a Domain" %}
**Overview**

A **Domain** is created to logically group related data assets under a common business area. It provides a structured way to organize information, making data easier to discover, manage, govern, and understand across the organization. Domains also help assign ownership, enforce data governance policies, and improve collaboration between business and technical teams.

***

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to add a domain:

1. On the **SCIKIQ Left Navigation Bar**, expand **Data Catalogue Manager**, and then click **Domain.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2126).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Domain** page is displayed.

2. On the **Domain** page, click the **Add Domain** button on the top right corner.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2125).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Add Domain** dialogue box is displayed.

3. On the **Add Domain** dialogue box, specify **Domain Name, Domain Description, Domain (Data Domain or Sub Domain), Parent Domain, Domain Owner, Delegate**, and then click the **Save** button.

> <mark style="color:red;">**Note:**</mark> Here is the description for **Data Domain, Subdomain,** and **Delegate:**
>
> * **Data Domain:** A **Data Domain** is a high-level business category that groups related data assets based on a common subject area, function, or business capability. **Example:** Customer, Finance, Sales, Human Resources.
> * **Subdomain:** A **Subdomain** is a more specific subdivision of a data domain that groups related data within a particular business area. **Example:** Within the Customer domain, subdomains may include Personal Information, Contact Details, and Purchase History.
> * **Delegate: Delegate** is the person who is authorized to act on behalf of the domain owner or steward.

{% tab title="Update a Domain" %}
**Overview**

A domain may need to be updated when its information, ownership, governance details, or business context changes over time.&#x20;

Updating a domain helps ensure that domain metadata remains accurate, relevant, and aligned with current organizational requirements.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to update an existing domain:

1. On the **Domains** page, click the **Edit** button corresponding to the required domain.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2132).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2133).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The domain is updated.
{% endtab %}

1. On the **Domains** page, click the **Delete** button corresponding to the required domain.
