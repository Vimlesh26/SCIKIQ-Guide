## Overview

The purpose of **adding a catalogue** is to register and organize data assets in a centralized repository so that users can easily discover, understand, and access relevant data. A catalogue serves as a structured inventory of datasets, making data management and governance more efficient across the organization.

***

## **Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to add a catalogue:

1. On the **SCIKIQ Left Navigation Bar**, expand **Data Catalogue Manager**, and then click **Catalogue.**

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2100).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Catalogue** page is displayed.

2. On the **Catalogue** page, click the **Add Catalogue** button on the top right corner.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2101).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Add Catalogue** dialogue box is displayed.

3. On the **Add Catalogue** dialogue box, specify **Catalogue Name, Catalogue Description, Entity, Domain, Catalogue Owner, Delegate**, and then click the **Save** button.

> <mark style="color:red;">**Note:**</mark> **Delegate** is the user who is authorized to act on behalf of the catalogue owner or steward.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2102).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The catalogue is added. See the below example image.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2103).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
