## Overview

As part of managing existing catalogues, you can perform a range of actions to maintain their accuracy, relevance, and governance.&#x20;

These actions include viewing catalogue information to understand its metadata and configuration, reviewing audit history to track changes and user activities, updating catalogue details to reflect evolving business or data requirements, viewing associated asset information to explore the datasets and resources linked to the catalogue, and deleting catalogues that are no longer required.&#x20;

Together, these capabilities help ensure that catalogues remain organized, up to date, and aligned with organizational data management objectives.

<summary><strong>Update Existing Catalogues</strong></summary>

**Overview**

A catalogue should be updated whenever any significant change occurs that impacts the accuracy, usability, governance, or discoverability of the data asset it represents.&#x20;

Updating a catalogue helps maintain a trustworthy and up-to-date inventory of data assets, enabling users to discover, understand, and use data effectively.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to update an existing catalogue:

1. On the **Catalogues** page, click the **View Catalogue Info** button corresponding to the required catalogue.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2117).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Edit** **Catalogue** page is displayed.

2. On the **Edit** **Catalogue** page, make the required changes, and then click the **Save** button at the bottom.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2118).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The catalogue is updated.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>View Associated Asset Information</strong></summary>

**Purpose**

The purpose of viewing associated asset information is to understand the data assets linked to a catalogue and assess their relevance, structure, and usage within the organization. It provides visibility into the datasets, tables, files, reports, or other resources that the catalogue represents.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to view associated asset information of an existing catalogue:

* On the **Catalogues** page, click the **View Asset Info** button corresponding to the required catalogue.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2119).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Catalogues Asset screen is displayed.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Delete Catalogues</strong></summary>

**Purpose**

A catalogue should be **deleted when it is no longer needed, relevant, or valid within the organization's data ecosystem**. Removing obsolete catalogues helps maintain an accurate and clutter-free data inventory.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to delete an existing catalogue:

1. On the **Catalogues** page, click the **Delete** button corresponding to the required catalogue.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2122).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Catalogue Delete** message box is displayed.

2. On the **Catalogue Delete** message box, review the message, and then click the **Yes** button.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2123).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The catalogue is deleted.

</details>
