### Overview

The **High-Level Data Quality (DQ) Dashboard** provides a consolidated view of data quality across the organization. It enables stakeholders to assess the overall health of data by presenting summary-level insights into Data Quality (DQ) measures, validation results, and quality trends across catalogs, domains, sub-domains, and entities.

<details>

<summary><strong>Purpose</strong></summary>

The High-Level DQ Dashboard provides a quick overview of the organization's data quality status, helping stakeholders identify areas that require attention and prioritize data quality improvement initiatives.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>What the Dashboard Displays</strong></summary>

The High-Level DQ Dashboard consists of the following views:

* **Overall Summary** – Displays a consolidated view of Data Quality (DQ) performance, including month-over-month pass rates and Business Data Element (BDE) coverage.
* **Summary Failed DQ** – Provides a summary of failed Data Quality (DQ) validations across the selected scope.
* **Failed Measure Analysis** – Presents a detailed analysis of failed DQ measures to help identify recurring issues and improvement areas.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Benefits</strong></summary>

The High-Level DQ Dashboard helps stakeholders monitor organizational data quality, identify trends and quality gaps, measure the effectiveness of Data Quality (DQ) initiatives, and support informed decision-making through reliable data quality insights.

</details>

### Using High-Level - DQ Dashboard

**Overview**

The **High-Level Data Quality (DQ) Dashboard** provides a consolidated overview of data quality measures across the organization. It enables you to view the **Overall Summary**, **Summary of Failed DQ**, including **Failed Measures by Entity**, **Month-on-Month Trend by Entity**, and **Failed Measures by Legal Entity, Catalogue, and Domain**.&#x20;

Additionally, you can analyze failed data quality measures in detail through the **Failed Measures Analysis** view.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to view **High-Level - DQ Dashboard**:

_**To use High-Level**_ _**-  DQ Dashboard**_

1. On the **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, under **SCIKIQ CONTROL**, expand **Data Quality Manager**, and then click **High-Level - DQ Dashboard**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2559).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **High-Level - DQ Dashboard** page is displayed.

2. On the **High-Level - DQ Dashboard** page:

A. Select the **Legal Entity, Catalogue, Domain, To Date** and **From Date.**

B. Click the **Search** button.&#x20;

The **High-Level- DQ Dashboard** data is displayed.

C. **To view the overall summary**, click the **Overall Summary** tab.

D. **To view the failed DQ**, click the **Summary Failed DQ** tab.

E. **To view the failed measure analysis**, click the **Failed Analysis** tab.
