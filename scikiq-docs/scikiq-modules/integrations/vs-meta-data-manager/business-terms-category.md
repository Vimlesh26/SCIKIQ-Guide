# Business Terms Category

### <mark style="color:blue;">What is a Business Terms Category?</mark>

A **Business Terms Category** is a way to classify and organize various business-related terms, concepts, or items into logical groups. These categories help businesses manage, track, and search for specific terms efficiently. Categories are particularly useful when dealing with large amounts of data that need to be organized, such as in content management systems, glossaries, or product catalogs.

Each **Business Term Category** typically has the following attributes:

* **Term Category Name**: The name of the category (e.g., BTC1, BTC2, etc.)
* **Description**: A short description that provides more details about the category's content.
* **Parent Category**: If the category is a sub-category, it will have a parent category.
* **Level**: The hierarchical level of the category (e.g., Level 1, Level 2).
* **Source**: The origin or source of the term or category (e.g., a specific business unit or department).

### <mark style="color:blue;">Why Are Business Terms Categories Important?</mark>

Business Terms Categories offer several key benefits:

* **Organization**: Grouping related terms together improves understanding and helps streamline searches.
* **Efficiency**: Simplifies management, especially in bulk data or extensive terminologies.
* **Scalability**: Makes it easier to add, modify, or remove terms without affecting other parts of the system.
* **Clarity**: Provides clearer descriptions and contexts for each term.

### <mark style="color:blue;">**Structure of Business Terms Categories**</mark>

#### Key Attributes of Each Business Term Category

1. **Term Category Name**
   * The **name** of the category.
   * This is the primary identifier used for referencing the category.
   * Example: `BTC1`, `BTC2`.
2. **Description**
   * A brief **description** that outlines what the category encompasses.
   * It should be short and to the point but provide enough context for users to understand the purpose of the category.
   * Example: "Business Terms related to Sales" or "Financial Terminology for Accounting."
3. **Parent Category**
   * The **parent category** is the category to which this one belongs if it's a sub-category.
   * For example, `BTC1` might be under a broader category called `Sales`.
4. **Level**
   * The **level** of the category indicates its position in the hierarchy.
   * Example:
     * Level 1: Top-level categories.
     * Level 2: Sub-categories under Level 1 categories.
     * Level 3: Sub-categories under Level 2 categories.
5. **Source**
   * The **source** refers to where the term category is derived from or which department or team is responsible for it.
   * Example: "Marketing Team," "Finance Department," or "HR."

### <mark style="color:blue;">**Adding a New Business Term Category**</mark>

To **add a new category**, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the Category Name**
   * Choose a unique name for the new category (e.g., `BTC5`, `HR_Terms`).
2. **Provide a Description**
   * Write a short description of what the category represents (maximum of 150 characters).
   * Example: "Business Terms related to Employee Benefits."
3. **Set the Parent Category**
   * If this category is a sub-category, you will need to specify the parent category under which it will fall.
   * Example: If the parent category is "HR," then the parent category will be selected as "HR."
4. **Select the Level**
   * Determine the **level** of the category.
   * Level 1 will typically be for high-level categories.
   * Level 2 and beyond will be for more granular sub-categories.
5. **Specify the Source**
   * Enter the **source** or department responsible for the category (e.g., "HR Team," "Finance Department").
6. **Save the Category**
   * Once all the fields are filled, save the new category. It will appear in the Business Terms Category table.

### <mark style="color:blue;">**Editing an Existing Category**</mark>

To **edit an existing category**, follow these steps:

1. **Select the Category**
   * In the table, find the category you want to edit.
   * Click the "Edit" button next to the category.
2. **Update the Category Details**
   * Change the category name, description, parent category, level, or source as necessary.
3. **Save the Changes**
   * After making the necessary updates, click the "Save" button to save the changes.

### <mark style="color:blue;">**Deleting a Business Term Category**</mark>

To **delete a category**, follow these steps:

1. **Select the Category**
   * In the Business Terms Category table, find the category you want to delete.
   * Click the "Delete" button next to the category.
2. **Confirm Deletion**
   * A confirmation message will appear asking if you're sure you want to delete the category.
   * Confirm the deletion.
3. **Category Removed**
   * Once confirmed, the category will be removed from the system.

### <mark style="color:blue;">**Bulk Uploading Business Terms Categories**</mark>

If you need to upload multiple categories at once, you can use the **bulk upload feature**. Here’s how to do it:

1. **Prepare the File**
   * Prepare your data in either a **CSV** or **Excel** format with the following columns:
     * **Term Category Name**
     * **Description**
     * **Parent Category**
     * **Level**
     * **Source**
2. **Upload the File**
   * Navigate to the **Bulk Upload** section in the system.
   * Select the file you prepared for upload.
3. **Review and Confirm**
   * The system will preview the data from the file. Review it to ensure everything looks correct.
4. **Complete the Upload**
   * Once confirmed, click "Upload" to add the categories in bulk.

#### 6. **Searching for Business Terms Categories**

You can easily search for categories by using the **Search Terms Category** functionality. Here’s how:

1. **Enter the Search Term**
   * Use the search bar to type the name or description of the category you’re looking for.
2. **View the Results**
   * The system will display any categories that match the search term.
3. **Apply Filters**
   * You can also filter by category name, parent category, level, or source for more targeted results.
