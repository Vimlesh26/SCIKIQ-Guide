# Glossary

### <mark style="color:blue;">**Overview**</mark>

A Business Glossary plays a vital role in any organization's data governance strategy. It serves as a centralized repository of well-defined, agreed-upon business terms and concepts that help align communication across business and technical teams. More than just a dictionary of terms, a business glossary maps out relationships between those terms and embeds them within the broader organizational context, ensuring clarity, reducing ambiguity, and facilitating informed decision-making.

SCIKIQ’s Business Glossary feature has been designed with both functionality and user experience in mind. It supports the creation, management, and governance of glossary terms through a user-friendly interface and provides powerful tools for collaboration, search, lineage analysis, and role-based access. With SCIKIQ, organizations can ensure consistent understanding and use of business terms across departments and systems, enabling unified data practices and promoting a data-literate culture.

### <mark style="color:blue;">**Objectives of a Business Glossary**</mark>

The primary purpose of a business glossary is to document an organization’s terminology and the relationships between those terms in a structured, searchable, and standardized format. A well-maintained glossary provides a single source of truth for business language, which in turn improves data quality, reduces misinterpretation, and aligns strategic objectives across departments. Key objectives include:

* **Creating a common language** across departments and teams to improve collaboration.
* **Preventing data misuse** due to inconsistent or misunderstood terminology.
* **Bridging the gap** between business and technical users by providing definitions and context for terms that affect data systems and reporting.
* **Centralizing knowledge** and promoting reuse of verified definitions, which fosters transparency and institutional memory.
* **Simplifying data governance** by making key business terms searchable, relatable, and actionable.

### <mark style="color:blue;">**Roles and Responsibilities**</mark>

In SCIKIQ, managing and using the Business Glossary is a collaborative effort involving various roles:

**Glossary Owners** are usually senior leaders or business unit heads with deep domain knowledge. They are not responsible for day-to-day glossary operations but ensure that the content aligns with business goals and maintains strategic relevance.

**Data Stewards** are responsible for creating and maintaining glossary content. They define terms, validate definitions, associate terms with policies or business rules, and ensure consistent usage across the enterprise. They also work closely with owners and stakeholders to resolve conflicts, update outdated definitions, and identify gaps.

**Stakeholders** are typically business users or subject matter experts who validate glossary content from a business perspective. They approve terms, suggest changes, and ensure that the glossary reflects the organization’s real-world needs and processes.

**Business Users**, such as analysts, managers, and decision-makers, use the glossary to understand data context and apply it in their reports, analyses, and workflows.

**Technical Users**, including data architects and developers, rely on the glossary to align data structures and processes with business definitions, thus reducing misalignment between systems and stakeholders.

### <mark style="color:blue;">**All Glossary View**</mark>

The “All Glossary” view is the central hub for accessing all glossary entries. Glossaries are grouped by categories such as Human Resources, Sales, Finance, or Technical, making it easy for users to locate relevant glossaries based on departmental needs. Each entry is displayed with essential details, and users can quickly search for a glossary using keywords or filter by category. This view serves as the launchpad for performing all actions, such as adding, editing, or analyzing glossaries.

### <mark style="color:blue;">**Creating a Glossary**</mark>

1.  **Navigate to Glossary Workspace**\
    Go to the Business Glossary section from the SCIKIQ main menu.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (340).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2.  **Click “+ Add Glossary”**\
    On the top right, click the **“+ Add Glossary”** button to open a pop-up form.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (341).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3.  **Enter Glossary Details**

    * **Glossary Name**: Provide a meaningful name.
    * **Category**: Select a relevant category (e.g., HR, Sales, Finance).
    * **Owner**: Assign a glossary owner (typically a business lead).
    * **Definition**: Write a clear and concise definition explaining the glossary's purpose.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (342).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4.  **Link Existing Terms**\
    Use the **search box** to find and link relevant business terms to ensure consistency and reuse.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (343).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
5. **Save the Glossary**\
   Click **“Save Glossary”** to publish it. The glossary will now be visible to users with access rights.
