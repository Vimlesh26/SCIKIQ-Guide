### Overview

The **Create Asset** feature allows users to generate reusable assets from an existing dashboard for easier access and management. These assets can be utilized across different modules or shared with other users based on business requirements. This feature helps improve reusability, reduce duplication of effort, and maintain consistency across reports and dashboards. It also enables efficient organization and management of dashboard-related resources. &#x20;

***

## Procedure

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to create assets:

1. On the **Dashboard List page**, click the **Create Asset** icon corresponding to the required dashboard.

<img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (249).png" alt="Dashboard - Create Asset Icon" height="288" width="624">

The Add Asset dialogue box is displayed.

2. On the **Add Asset** dialogue box, enter the required details, and then click the **Next** button.

<img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (250).png" alt="Dashboard List - Add Asset Dialogue Box" height="271" width="624">

3. On the next page, enter the **Asset** description, and then click the **Submit** button.

The asset is created.
