### Overview

The **View Change History** feature allows users to track and review modifications made to a dashboard after its creation. It provides visibility into changes such as updates to configurations, widgets, layouts, filters, and other dashboard settings. This feature helps maintain an audit trail, making it easier to identify what changes were made and when they occurred. It also supports better monitoring, troubleshooting, and version tracking of dashboard updates. &#x20;

## Procedure

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to view change history:

1. On the **Dashboard List page**, click the **Update** icon corresponding to the required dashboard.

2. On the **Change History** page:

* Use the **Filters** or **Quick Select** options to view the change history of a required dashboard.
* Click on a **row** to view the modification details.
