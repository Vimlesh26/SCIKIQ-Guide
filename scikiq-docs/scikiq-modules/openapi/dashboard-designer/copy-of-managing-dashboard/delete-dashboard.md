### Overview

The **Delete Dashboard** feature allows removing dashboards that are no longer required or relevant. This helps maintain a clean and organized dashboard repository by eliminating outdated or unused dashboards. Removing unnecessary dashboards also reduces clutter and improves overall system management. It is important to verify the dashboard details before deletion to avoid accidental removal of important data or configurations.

***

## Procedure

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to delete a dashboard:

1. On the **Dashboard List page**, click the **Delete** icon corresponding to the required dashboard.

<img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (253).png" alt="Dashboard List - Delete Icon" height="288" width="624">

The Delete Notification is displayed.

2. On the **Delete Notification**, click the **Yes** button.

The dashboard is deleted.
