### Overview

The **View or Hide a Column** feature allows controlling the visibility of columns within a table based on their requirements. It enables displaying only the relevant columns and hide unnecessary information, resulting in a cleaner and more focused view of the data. This feature improves readability and enhances the user experience by reducing visual clutter. It also provides flexibility in customizing the table display according to specific business or reporting needs.

***

## Procedure

> <mark style="color:red;">**Note:**</mark> On the **Dashboard List** page, the column headers displayed in **blue** indicate **visible** columns, whereas the tabs displayed in **grey** represent **hidden** columns.

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to view or hide a column:

1. On the **Dashboard List** page, if you want to hide a column, click on that column header.&#x20;
2. If you want to view it, click again on the column header.
