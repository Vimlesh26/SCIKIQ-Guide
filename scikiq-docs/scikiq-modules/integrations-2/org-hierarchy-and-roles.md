# Org Hierarchy & Roles

### 1. Introduction

**The Organizational Hierarchy & Roles** feature in **ScikIQ** is designed to ensure that every user has a **clearly defined scope of responsibility, access level, and authority**.

Without structured roles, organizations risk:

* Unauthorized data access
* Confusion in task ownership
* Weak governance and audit failures

By assigning **hierarchical roles**, ScikIQ ensures:

* Clear governance from executives down to operational users
* Strong data security and controlled access
* Efficient workflows, avoiding duplication of effort
* Accountability through role-based responsibilities

### 2. Organizational Hierarchy in ScikIQ

IIn ScikIQ, the organizational hierarchy is designed to **streamline workflows** and establish **clear responsibilities**.

* Starts from **high-level roles** (Client Admin, CDO)
* Moves down to **functional roles** (Data Engineer, Report Designer, etc.)
* Ends with **operational roles** like Viewer

This hierarchy ensures a **structured approach to governance, analytics, and operations**.

### **Concept of Entities**

In ScikIQ, an entity represents a logical grouping of data and resources that pertain to a specific domain or subject area within the organization. Entities help in categorizing and segregating data environments to streamline management, access, and operations. Each entity can have specific attributes, roles, and permissions associated with it, enabling administrators to effectively control data access and functionality based on the organizational structure and business requirements. This concept is pivotal for maintaining data integrity, fostering collaboration, and ensuring compliance with organizational policies.

### 4. Understanding Role-based Access Controls in ScikIQ

In ScikIQ, Role-based Access Controls (RBAC) offer a systematic way of regulating user access to various resources based on their assigned roles. Each role is associated with specified entities and carries unique permissions. This ensures that users can only interact with the data and functions necessary for their responsibilities.

### **Key Features of RBAC in ScikIQ**

* **Multiple Role Assignments:** Users can hold multiple roles, each potentially linked to different entities, enabling flexibility in access according to varied responsibilities.
* **Permissions Management:** Every role is defined by its set of permissions, which dictates the type of access (read, write, modify) a user has over resources.
* **Centralized Policy Control:** Policy management aligns with organizational data and governance strategies, ensuring compliance and secure data handling.

The combination of these features ensures that ScikIQ provides robust, secure, and tailored access control to support effective data management and operations.
