# Entity

## Concept of Entity

*   ### Entity Hierarchy Setup in ScikIQ

    ABC PLC, a global enterprise with offices in APAC, Europe, and North America, has acquired a ScikIQ license. They can configure their Entity hierarchy in ScikIQ to mirror their legal entity structure.


* **Default Entity**

The default entity in ScikIQ is the licensing corporation's name. Data Assets can either be stored directly under this default entity or arranged in more detailed hierarchies, enabling precise data organization.

*   #### Departments under Legal Entities

    Entities in ScikIQ serve both as licensing and data organization structures. For each legal entity, departments such as Finance, Marketing, Sales, and Operations can be established.
*   #### Group Level Department

    Additionally, a Head-Office can be established as a department at the group level, offering a centralized management structure.
