# Business Terms

### <mark style="color:blue;">Overview</mark>

In today’s data-driven world, organizations increasingly recognize the need to standardize and govern business terminology. Inconsistent or vague terms can lead to confusion, operational errors, and compliance issues.

**SCIKIQ’s Business Terms Management Interface** addresses this challenge with a centralized, no-code platform for defining, managing, and governing business terms. It enables teams across departments—finance, marketing, IT, HR, and more—to standardize terminology, track usage, and maintain a single source of truth aligned with organizational goals.

#### **Why Data Governance Matters in Business Terms Management**

Effective data governance ensures consistency, transparency, and regulatory compliance. Without it, departments may define terms differently, leading to miscommunication and costly mistakes—especially in regulated industries like finance, healthcare, and insurance.

SCIKIQ’s platform supports data governance by enabling organizations to:

* Maintain consistent definitions across systems and teams
* Link terms to regulatory standards for easier compliance
* Foster collaboration with a shared, standardized vocabulary

#### **Why Choose SCIKIQ's Business Terms Management Interface?**

SCIKIQ offers several strategic advantages:

* **Centralized Management**: All business terms reside in one repository for easy access and modification.
* **Governance and Compliance**: Terms can be assigned roles, statuses, and approvals to support regulatory and internal governance.
* **User-Friendly Interface**: Even non-technical users can add, manage, and search terms with ease.
* **Scalability and Flexibility**: Suitable for organizations of all sizes, it scales to meet evolving business needs.

**Now, let’s explore the features of the business terms interface in detail.**

### <mark style="color:blue;">**Features of the business terms**</mark>

#### **1. Term Name**

**Purpose:** Acts as the unique identifier for a business term.

**Key Features:**

* **Unique Naming:** Ensures every term is distinct and recognizable.
* **Clarity:** Names like "Supplier Name" are intuitive and descriptive.
* **Standardization:** Promotes consistent terminology across systems.

#### **2. Definition**

**Purpose:** Provides a clear, concise explanation to eliminate ambiguity.

**Key Features:**

* **Contextual Clarity:** Explains exactly what the term represents.
* **Consistency:** Ensures all users interpret the term uniformly.
* **Documentation:** Helps onboard new users and supports governance.

#### **3. Purpose**

**Purpose:** Describes the term’s value in business operations.

**Key Features:**

* **Business Alignment:** Links terms to strategic goals.
* **Use Context:** Explains how the term supports workflows or decision-making.
* **Traceability:** Shows the practical relevance of the term.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1455).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3. Fill in the "**Terms Name**" field with the name of the business term.
4. Provide a definition for the term in the corresponding field.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1463).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
6.  **Select Business Term Details:**

    1. **Business Process:** Use the dropdown menu to select the relevant business process associated with the term.
    2. **Owner:** Choose an individual or team responsible for the term from the "Process Owner" dropdown.
    3. **Catalogue**: Select the appropriate catalog (e.g. "EDW Catalogue") to organize the term under
    4. **Glossary:** Optionally link the term to a glossary if available.

7\.  **Specify Additional Attributes:**

Category: Use the dropdown to categorize the term appropriately.

* Sensitive: Indicate whether the term contains sensitive data by selecting the appropriate option.
*   Classification: Define the classification for the term (e.g. "Public," "Confidential").

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1465).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

10\.  Review and Save:

* After filling out all the necessary fields, review the information to ensure accuracy.
* Use the "Clear" button to reset fields if needed.
* Click the "Add" button to save the business term.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1462).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

11\.  View Added Terms:

### <mark style="color:blue;">Bulk Upload</mark>&#x20;

The Bulk Upload feature in SCIKIQ’s Business Terms module simplifies the addition of multiple terms at once, making it especially valuable for organizations managing large or complex datasets. This functionality significantly enhances efficiency by reducing manual input, allowing users to onboard new terms or update existing ones in bulk. As a result, it supports a more streamlined and scalable approach to business term management—ensuring consistency, saving time, and strengthening overall data governance. Here's how the process works:

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1069).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Bulk Upload </p></figcaption></figure>

**Steps to Use the Bulk Upload**

1.  Navigate to the Bulk Upload tab in the Business Terms interface.



    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1480).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2.  Click on the Download Template File link to obtain a pre-configured Excel template.

    &#x20;

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1481).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    1. Open the downloaded template, which includes predefined fields for term detail.
    2.  Provide the N number of terms you wish to upload. Each row represents a business term, and fields include - Term Name, Definition, Purpose, Category, Process Owner.

        <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (387).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### <mark style="color:blue;">Share Business Term</mark>

Sharing a business term ensures visibility and controlled collaboration across teams.

#### Steps to Share a Business Term:

1.  **Open Business Terms Interface**

    * Click on **Business Terms** from the dashboard.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (379).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2.  **Find the Term to Share**

    * Use the **search bar** or **filters** to locate the specific term.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (378).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3. **Click the Share Icon**
   * Click the **Share** button (usually represented with a share or permission icon) next to the term.
4. **Configure Sharing Settings**
   * A **Resource Permission** pop-up will appear.
   * Choose visibility level:
     * **Public**: Everyone can view.
     * **Private**: Only the creator or specified users can view.
     * **Shared**: Selected users or groups can access.
5.  **Assign Access**

    * In the **Share With** dropdown:
      * Choose **User** or **Group**.
      * Select the specific user/group from the secondary dropdown.
      * Assign a **Role** (e.g., Viewer, Contributor).
    * Click **Add More** to include additional users or groups.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (380).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
6.  **Save Sharing Settings**

    * Click **Save** to apply the permissions.
    * Click **Close** to exit without changes.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (381).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
