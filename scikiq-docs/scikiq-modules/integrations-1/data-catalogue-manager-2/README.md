### Overview

The **Data Catalogue Manager** is a system or tool that helps organize, manage, and maintain the metadata of datasets. It provides a structured way to handle data assets across various domains and subdomains.

The primary purpose of a Data Catalogue Manager is to answer questions such as:

* What business data exists?
* How is it organized?
* Where can I find specific business information?
* How are different business concepts related?

***

### Key Components

The Data Catalogue Manager consists of the following key components.&#x20;

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Catalogue" %}
Catalogue is the highest-level organizational container within the Data Catalogue Manager. It represents a broad collection of related business information and serves as the entry point for organizing data.

**Purpose**

* Provides a top-level structure
* Organizes business information logically
* Enable easier navigation and discovery
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Domain" %}
Domain represents a business subject area or functional area within a catalogue. Domains group together related business entities that belong to the same business function.

**Purpose**

* Groups related business concepts
* Reflects organizational business areas
* Simplifies data organization
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Entity" %}
An Entity is a specific business object or business concept within a domain. Entities represent the things about which information is collected and managed.

**Purpose**

* Provides a structured representation of key business objects
* Organizes related information under a common business concept
* Helps users understand and navigate business information within a domain
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
