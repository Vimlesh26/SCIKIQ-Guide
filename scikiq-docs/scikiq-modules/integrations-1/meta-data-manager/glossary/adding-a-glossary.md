## Overview

The **Add Glossary** feature enables you to create a glossary for organizing related business terms and their definitions. A glossary provides a structured repository of business terminology, helping users easily locate, understand, and manage business concepts. Creating glossaries promotes consistency, improves collaboration, and supports effective metadata management and data governance.

***

## Procedure

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to add a glossary:

_**To add a glossary**_

1. On the **SCIKIQ Left Navigation Bar**, go to **SCIKIQ Control**, and then expand **Meta Data Manager**.

&#x20;     The Meta Data Manager menu is displayed.

2. On the **Meta Data Manager** menu, click **Glossary.**

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2223).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **All Glossaries** page is displayed.

3. On the **All Glossaries** page, click the **Add Glossary** button on the top right corner of the page.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2224).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Business Glossary dialogue box is displayed.

4. On the **Business Glossary** dialogue box:

A. Specify the **Glossary Name, Glossary Category, Owner**, and **Definition** of Glossary.

B. In the **Search Business Terms** field, enter the required **business term**. For example, Customer.

The **Results** section is displayed with the results related to the entered business term.

C. From the **Results** section, select the **relevant result** by clicking the ![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2226).png>) icon corresponding to it.

D. Finally, click the **Save** button.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2225).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The glossary is added. See the below example image.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2227).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
