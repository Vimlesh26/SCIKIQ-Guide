## Overview

An entity is created to represent a key business object, concept, or subject area within the organization's data ecosystem, such as a Customer, Product, Employee, or Order.&#x20;

Entities help organize and structure business data by defining its attributes, relationships, ownership, and governance information.&#x20;

Creating entities improves data discoverability, consistency, and understanding, while supporting effective metadata management, data governance, and business process alignment.

## **Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to add an  entity:

1. On the **SCIKIQ Left Navigation Bar**, expand **Data Catalogue Manager**, and then click **Entity.**

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2153).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Entity** page is displayed.

2. On the  **Entity** page, click the **Create Organizational Entity** button on the top right corner.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2154).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Create Entity** dialogue box is displayed.

3. On the **Create Entity** dialogue box, specify **Entity Name, Type, Parent Entity,** and then click the **Save** button.

See the below example image.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2155).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The entity is created. See the below image.
