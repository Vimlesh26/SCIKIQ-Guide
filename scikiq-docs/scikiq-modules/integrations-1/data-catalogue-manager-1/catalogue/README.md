## Overview

Data Catalogs are a core component of the ScikIQ Business Data Platform, providing a centralized repository for discovering, organizing, and managing enterprise data assets. They help users quickly find relevant datasets, understand their context, and determine their suitability for business and analytical purposes.

ScikIQ Data Catalogs help inventory and classify distributed data assets, reduce data sprawl, and provide visibility into information supply chains across the enterprise. Leveraging machine learning (ML), they automate key metadata management activities such as metadata discovery, ingestion, enrichment, classification, and relationship mapping.

By providing business context and improving data discoverability, Data Catalogs empower data stewards, analysts, engineers, scientists, and business users to make better use of organizational data and derive greater business value from data and analytics initiatives.
