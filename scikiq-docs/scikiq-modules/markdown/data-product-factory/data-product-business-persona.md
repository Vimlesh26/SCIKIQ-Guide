# Data Product/Business Persona

### **What is a Data Product/Business Persona in Scikiq?**

In Scikiq, a **Data Product** refers to an application or tool that leverages data and analytics to drive business decisions and insights. It is crafted to be intuitive and engaging, ensuring that stakeholders can leverage data effectively without deep technical knowledge. A **Business Persona** in this context is a representation of a typical user or stakeholder who interacts with the data product. It embodies their requirements, preferences, and workflow to enhance the product's relevance and usability.

### **Key Features:**

* **Intuitive Interface:** Designed for ease of use, enabling users to quickly access and interpret data.
* **Customizable Views:** Allows users to tailor dashboards to highlight the most relevant metrics for their business needs.
* **Automated Reports:** Offers scheduled reporting and alerts to keep users informed of critical changes or achievements.
* **Integration Capabilities:** Seamlessly connects with various data sources to provide a comprehensive view.
* **Real-Time Updates:** Ensures data is refreshed regularly to provide the most current insights.
* **Role-Based Access Control:** Provides secure data access, ensuring users only see data relevant to their role.

Example:

* **Marketing Manager Persona:** Needs insights into campaign performance, audience engagement, and ROI metrics, all available in a clean and easily navigable dashboard.

## Use Case

### Prompt-1

I want to provide physical security services to old age people who are not mentally and physically alert. If from sensors there is a signal of security breach, it should trigger alarm and raise concern to old age home resident.

### Prompt-2

To enhance physical security for elderly residents who may be mentally or physically less alert, the system can integrate sensors to detect breaches and automatically trigger alarms, notifying both the residents and on-duty caregivers. In critical situations, alerts can be extended to family members or emergency contacts. Residents could wear devices with panic buttons, allowing them to directly alert caregivers if they feel unsafe. Additionally, real-time location tracking through these wearables can help caregivers quickly respond to any emergency, ensuring swift assistance and continuous monitoring within the facility.

&#x20;

The Telco Data Marketplace will enable seamless data exchange between enterprise customers, residents, and intermediaries, specifically focusing on use cases such as elderly care and connected homes. Residents, particularly those in elderly care or smart homes, will have full control over their personal data, allowing them to manage consent and privacy preferences. The marketplace will support various types of data, including usage patterns from connected devices, behavioral data, health metrics, and operational information from smart home systems.

&#x20;

Enterprise customers, such as elderly care facilities and healthcare providers, will be able to securely access anonymized and aggregated data to enhance resident care, monitor health conditions, and optimize operational efficiency. Intermediaries like data brokers will enrich this data, increasing its value and market liquidity while ensuring transparency and compliance with privacy regulations such as GDPR and HIPAA.

&#x20;

Key functional requirements include ensuring residents' control over their data, enabling secure and compliant data sharing, and supporting insights for elderly care providers to deliver personalized, AI-driven services. Non-functional requirements focus on maintaining high levels of privacy, security, scalability, and regulatory compliance in a trusted and ethical data-sharing environment.
