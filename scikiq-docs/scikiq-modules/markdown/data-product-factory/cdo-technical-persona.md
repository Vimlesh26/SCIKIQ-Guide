# CDO/Technical Persona

### CDO/Technical Persona in Scikiq

In Scikiq, a CDO/Technical Persona is responsible for overseeing data governance and ensuring that data policies are aligned with the organization's goals. This role involves leading the integration of new technologies to boost data analytics capabilities and collaborating with cross-functional teams to enhance data workflows and infrastructure. The persona also focuses on strategic initiatives to improve data quality and accessibility, ensuring robust data management practices are in place.

### Step 1: Explore metadata

Explore the metadata to understand the structure, relationships, and details of the data model. This helps in identifying key entities, attributes, and their connections, providing a foundation for effective data management and utilization.

### Step 2: Get the Data Product Use Case from the Chat Window

Use the chat window to define and retrieve the data product use case based on the data. This will help in aligning the product with the specific business requirements and objectives, ensuring the correct data is processed and utilized

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (853).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Step 3: Suggested Use Case

Based on the data and requirements, the system will suggest a relevant use case for your data product. This suggestion will guide the development and deployment process, ensuring the product meets the specific needs of your business or application.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (854).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Step 4: Choose the Product

Select the product that best suits your needs. This will prompt you to the data product registration screen, where the process for registration and deployment will follow the same steps as mentioned earlier.
