# Data Product Factory

**Data Product Factory** is a framework that simplifies the creation and management of data products tailored for business and technical personas. It bridges the gap between raw data and actionable insights, ensuring data is aligned with organizational goals.

1. **Data Product/Business Persona**: Focuses on creating data products for business users, providing actionable insights through intuitive, user-friendly interfaces. It emphasizes ease of use and relevance for decision-making.
2. **CDO/Technical Persona**: Tailored for Chief Data Officers (CDOs) and technical teams, enabling them to build, manage, and optimize data products with advanced configurations and technical precision.
3. **Product List**: A centralized catalog of all data products, allowing easy access, management, and tracking. It ensures transparency and organization across the data ecosystem.

**Data Product Factory** empowers organizations to transform raw data into structured, usable products that cater to both business and technical needs, driving efficiency and better decision-making.
