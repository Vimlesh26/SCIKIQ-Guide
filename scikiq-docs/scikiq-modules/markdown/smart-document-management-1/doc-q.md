# Doc Q

### **Doc Q (Document Quality)**&#x20;

#### **What is Doc Q?**

**Doc Q** stands for **Document Quality**, and in the context of **Smart Profiling**, it refers to the **automated assessment and validation** of documents based on structure, readability, completeness, format compliance, and extractability.

#### **Purpose of Doc Q in Smart Profiling**

To **ensure uploaded or ingested documents are usable**, complete, and meet predefined standards before they are consumed in business processes, semantic models, or AI workflows.

#### **Procurement Agent**

A **Procurement Agent** is a professional responsible for **sourcing, purchasing, and managing the supply of goods or services** on behalf of an organization. They act as the **link between the company and suppliers**, ensuring that all purchases are **cost-effective, timely, and compliant** with quality and contractual standards.
