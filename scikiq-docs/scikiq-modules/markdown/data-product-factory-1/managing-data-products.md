# Managing Data Products

***

### Instructions for Using this Page

* Click on an image to view it in a larger and clearer format.
* The inputs shown in the images are for example purposes only. You must enter the correct and actual data.

***

### Overview

Once a data product is created, it is displayed on the **Product List** page. From this page, you can manage the data product by viewing its **resource details** and **marketplace deployments**, as well as **updating** its information or **deleting** it when it is no longer required.&#x20;

The below **Procedure** section provide detailed instructions on how to perform each of these tasks:

***

### Procedure

Click on the below tabs to o view step-by-step instructions:

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Viewing Resource Details" %}
**Overview**

Viewing the **Resource Details** of a data product helps users understand the underlying resources and information associated with that product. It provides greater visibility into the data product and helps users determine whether it is relevant to their requirements.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to view resource details of a product:

**To view resource details**

1. On the SCIKIQ **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, expand **Data Product Factory** under **ML/GENAI STUDIO**, and then click **Product List.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2665).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Product List** page is displayed.&#x20;

2. On the **Product List** page, click the **Resources** button corresponding to the required data product.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2666).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Resource Details screen is displayed. See the image below.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2667).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Viewing Market Place Deployments" %}
**Overview**

**Viewing Marketplace Deployments** allows you to track and review where the data product has been deployed and made available in the Marketplace, helping you monitor its deployment status, availability, and usage across different environments or consumers.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to view market place deployments of a product:

**To view market place deployments**

1. On the SCIKIQ **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, expand **Data Product Factory** under **ML/GENAI STUDIO**, and then click **Product List.**
2. On the **Product List** page, click the **Marketplace Deployments** button corresponding to the required data product.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2604).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Marketplace Deployments** screen is displayed. See the image below:

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2605).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Updating a Data Product" %}
**Overview**

The need to **update a data product** arises when its underlying data, metadata, business requirements, rules, or other configurations change, or when the product needs to be improved to meet new or evolving user needs. Updating the data product helps keep it accurate, relevant, reliable, and aligned with current business requirements, while ensuring that users continue to access the most appropriate and up-to-date data product.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to update a product:

**To update a product**

1. On the SCIKIQ **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, expand **Data Product Factory** under **ML/GENAI STUDIO**, and then click **Product List.**
2. On the **Product List** page, click the **Edit** icon corresponding to the required data product.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2606).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Data Product** page is displayed.

3. On the **Data Product** page, make the required modifications, and then click the **Update Data Product** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2607).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Save Data Product screen is displayed.

4. On the **Save Data Product** screen, click the **Save** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2609).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Data product saved** successfully message is displayed.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Deleting a Data Product" %}
**Overview**

**Deleting a product** from the **Product List** helps remove data products that are no longer needed, outdated, duplicated, or created by mistake. This keeps the product list organized and easier to manage, making it simpler for users to find and work with the relevant data products.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to delete a product:

**To update a product**

1. On the SCIKIQ **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, expand **Data Product Factory** under **ML/GENAI STUDIO**, and then click **Product List.**
2. On the **Product List** page, click the **Delete** icon corresponding to the required data product.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2610).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Delete Data Product?** message box is displayed.

3. On the **Delete Data Product?** message box, click the **Yes** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2612).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The product is deleted from the Product List page.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Viewing/Hiding Columns" %}
**Overview**

The **View or Hide Columns** feature allows users to customize the page by displaying only the columns that are relevant to their needs and hiding unnecessary ones. This helps reduce clutter, improves readability, and makes it easier to focus on the most important information.

> <mark style="color:red;">**Note:**</mark> On the **Product List** page, column headings displayed in **blue** indicate visible columns, while those displayed in **grey** are hidden columns.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to view or hide a column:

_**To view or hide a column:**_

1. On the SCIKIQ **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, expand **Data Product Factory** under **ML/GENAI STUDIO**, and then click **Product List.**
2. On the **Product List** page, click on a **visible column header** displayed in blue color. For example, Data Product Name.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2613).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The column is hidden. See the below image in which the Data Product Name column is hidden.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2614).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. To view it again, click on the column header in **grey color**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2615).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The column is visible now. See the below image.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2616).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endtab %}
