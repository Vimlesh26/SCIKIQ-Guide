# Data Product Factory

***

### Overview

The **Data Product Factory** module in SCIKIQ provides a centralized platform for creating, managing, and publishing data products. It enables organizations to transform raw data into trusted, reusable data products that support business operations and data-driven decision-making.

The Data Product Factory helps organizations standardize the creation and management of data products, improve data accessibility, promote data reuse, and deliver trusted data products that support informed business decisions.

***

### Core Functionality

The **Data Product Factory** module enables **Business and Technical Personas** to create data products efficiently using the **SCIKIQ Chatbot**. It guides users through the data product creation process by helping them define business requirements or use cases and identify the relevant data and resources required to build the product. Once created, data products can be **registered and deployed**, making them available for other users to discover, access, and consume.

In addition to creating and deploying data products, the module provides capabilities to **manage data products and bundles** throughout their lifecycle, including viewing, updating, organizing, and maintaining them as business requirements evolve.

Refer to the **Related Topics** section below for detailed information about the key functionalities available in the **Data Product Factory** module.
