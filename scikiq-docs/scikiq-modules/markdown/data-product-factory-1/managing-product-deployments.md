# Managing Product Deployments

***

### Instructions for Using this Page

* Click on an image to view it in a larger and clearer format.
* The inputs shown in the images are for example purposes only. You must enter the correct and actual data.

***

### Overview

Once a product is deployed, it is displayed on the **Deployment List** page, where you can view the details of each deployment and copy its **Deployment Key** when required for accessing or managing the deployed product. The following **Procedure** section provides detailed instructions on how to perform each of these tasks.

***

### Procedure

Click on the below tabs to view step-by-step instructions:

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Viewing Deployment Details" %}
**Overview**

**Viewing** **deployment details** helps you review important information about a deployed product, such as **general information, timeline, deployment result,** and **error details**. This helps you verify that the product has been deployed correctly and easily monitor or manage the deployment when needed.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to view deployment details:

_**To view deployment details:**_

1. On the **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, expand **Data Product Factory** under **ML/GENAI STUDIO**, and then click **Data Product Deployments.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2626).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Deployment List** page is displayed.

2. On the **Deployment List** page, click the **View Details** button corresponding to the required deployment.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2627).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Deployment Details** screen is displayed. See the below image.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2628).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Copying Deployment Copy" %}
**Overview**

Copying the **Deployment Key** provides a quick way to obtain the unique identifier associated with a deployment. It may be required when accessing, integrating, identifying, or managing the deployed product in other parts of the system or through external applications.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to copy deployment key:

_**To copy deployment key**_

1. On the **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, expand **Data Product Factory** under **ML/GENAI STUDIO**, and then click **Data Product Deployments.**

The **Deployment List** page is displayed.&#x20;

2. On the **Deployment List** page, click the **Copy** **Full** **Key** icon corresponding to the required deployment.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2629).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Deployment** **Copied** message is displayed.&#x20;
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Viewing or Hiding Columns" %}
**Overview**

The **View or Hide Columns** feature allows users to customize the page by displaying only the columns that are relevant to their needs and hiding unnecessary ones. This helps reduce clutter, improves readability, and makes it easier to focus on the most important information.

> <mark style="color:red;">**Note:**</mark> On the **Deployment List** page, column headings displayed in **blue** indicate visible columns, while those displayed in **grey** are hidden columns.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to view or hide a column:

_**To view or hide a column:**_

1. On the **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, expand **Data Product Factory** under **ML/GENAI STUDIO**, and then click **Data Product Deployments.**&#x20;

The **Deployment List** page is displayed.&#x20;

2. On the **Deployment List** page, click on a **visible column header** displayed in blue color. For example, Deployment Key.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2632).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The column is hidden. See the below image in which the Data Deployment Key is hidden.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2633).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. To view it again, click on the column header in **grey color**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2634).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The column is visible now. See the below image.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2635).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
