# Registering a Data Product

***

### Instructions for Using this Page

* Click on an image to view it in a larger and clearer format.
* The inputs shown in the images are for example purposes only. You must enter the correct and actual data.

***

### Overview

The process of registering a data product involves a series of steps to define and publish the required product details. These steps include specifying _Product Information, SLA Information, Pricing Information, and Marketplace Information._

The below Procedure section provides a detailed description of each of these:

***

### Procedure

Click on the below tabs to view step-by-step instructions.

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Enter your product information" %}
1. On the **Data Product Use Case** page, click the **Register Data Product** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2643).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Register Product on Marketplace** screen is displayed.

2. On the **Register Product on Marketplace** screen, in the **Product Information** section, enter **Name, Support Email,** and **Description.**
3. If required, enable the **Bundle Products** option.
4. Click the **Next** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2644).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The SLA section is displayed.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Specify SLA Information" %}
1. On the **SLA** section, specify the **Response Time** and **Support Level.**
2. Click the **Next** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2645).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Pricing section is displayed.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Specify Pricing " %}
1. On the **Pricing** section, from the **Plan Type** drop-down box, select **Basic, Standard, Premium** or **Enterprise.**
2. From the **Currency** drop-down box, select the required **currency** type. For example, USD.
3. Click the **Next** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2650).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Register Product section is displayed.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Specify Marketplace Information" %}
**Overview**

1. On the **Register Product** section, from the **Marketplace** drop-down box, select the required **marketplace**. For example, SCIKIQ Marketplace.

> <mark style="color:red;">**Note:**</mark> You specify multiple marketplaces.

2. Click the **Finish** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2649).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Data Product Marketplace Registration message box is displayed. and the Data Product is registered.


{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
