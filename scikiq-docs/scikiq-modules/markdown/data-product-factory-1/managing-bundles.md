# Managing Bundles

***

### Instructions for Using this Page

* Click on an image to view it in a larger and clearer format.
* The inputs shown in the images are for example purposes only. You must enter the correct and actual data.

***

### Overview

Once a bundle is created, it is displayed on the **BundleList** page. From this page, you can manage the bundles by **updating price, updating bundle details,** as well as **deleting** it when it is no longer required.&#x20;

The below **Procedure** section provide detailed instructions on how to perform each of these tasks:

***

### Procedure

Click on the below tabs to o view step-by-step instructions:

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Updating a Bundle Price" %}
**Overview**

**Updating a bundle price** helps keep its pricing accurate and aligned with its current value and business strategy. The need may arise when the bundle's contents, costs, and pricing policies, etc.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to update pricing of a bundle:

_**To update a bundle**_

1. On the SCIKIQ **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, expand **Data Product Factory** under **ML/GENAI STUDIO**, and then click **Bundle List.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2621).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Bundle List** page is displayed.

2. On the **Bundle List** page, click the **Edit Pricing** button corresponding to the required bundle.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2622).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Edit Pricing for Bundle** screen is displayed.

3. On the **Edit Pricing for Bundle** screen:

A. From the **Plan Type** drop-down box, select the required **plan** choosing from **Standard, Premium**, and **Enterprise.**

> <mark style="color:red;">**Note:**</mark> You can any one, more, or all the plans.

B. From the **Currency** drop-down box, select the **required currency**. For example, USD.

C. If required, make changes to **No. of APIs, No. of Users, Throttling, One Time Charge,** and **Recurring Charge.**

C. If required, change the **Support** from **Email** to **Phone, Portal** or **Dedicated**.

D. Click the **Save** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2623).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Changes updated successfully** message is displayed.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Updating a Bundle Details" %}
**Overview**

Updating a **bundle** helps keep its contents and information current by allowing you to modify its details or **add, remove,** or **update the data products** included in it. This ensures that the bundle remains relevant, accurate, and aligned with changing business requirements and user needs..

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to update a bundle:

_**To update a bundle**_

1. On the SCIKIQ **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, expand **Data Product Factory** under **ML/GENAI STUDIO**, and then click **Bundle List.**

The **Bundle List** page is displayed.&#x20;

2. On the **Bundle List** page, click the **Edit Bundle** button corresponding to the required bundle.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2618).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Edit Bundle** page is displayed.&#x20;

3. On the **Edit Bundle** page:

A. If required, make changes to **Bundle Name, Bundle Description, Support Email,** and **Support Phone**.

B. **If you want to add a product** to the bundle, click the ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2619).png>)button corresponding to the required product.

C. **If you want to remove a product** from the bundle, click **X** button corresponding to the required product.

D. Click the **Update Bundle** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2620).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The bundle is updated.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Deleting a Bundle" %}
**Overview**

**Removing a bundle** from the **Bundle List** page helps eliminate bundles that are no longer required, outdated, duplicated, or created by mistake, keeping the list organized and making it easier to manage and find relevant bundles.

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to delete a bundle:

_**To delete a bundle**_

1. On the SCIKIQ **SCIKIQ Left Menu**, expand **Data Product Factory** under **ML/GENAI STUDIO**, and then click **Bundle List.**

The **Bundle List** page is displayed.&#x20;

2. On the **Bundle List** page, click the **Delete Bundle** button corresponding to the required bundle.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2624).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Delete Bundle?** message box is displayed.

2. On the **Delete Bundle?** message box, click the **Delete** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2625).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The bundle is deleted.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
