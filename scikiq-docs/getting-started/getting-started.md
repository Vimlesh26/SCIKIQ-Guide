{% tab title="About SCIKIQ" %}
**Overview**

SCIK<mark style="color:blue;">**IQ**</mark>  is a powerful **Data Management** application that brings together data management, governance, and transformation into a single, unified ecosystem. Designed as a **no-code, AI-powered solution**, it enables you to easily connect, curate, consume, and govern data—without the complexity of traditional data platforms.

Unlike conventional tools that rely on multiple disconnected solutions or require heavy technical effort, SCIK<mark style="color:blue;">**IQ**</mark> provides a **true no-code business data platform**. It helps organizations execute their data strategy efficiently and build a robust Data Management without dealing with underlying technical complexities.

{% tab title="Register as a Merchant" %}
**Overview**

A **Merchant** is a licensed user who can access and use the services provided by the **SCIK**<mark style="color:blue;">**IQ**</mark>**&#x20;web application**. Depending on your usage requirements, SCIKIQ offers two types of Merchant licenses.

| Individual License                              | Enterprise License                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Designed for a single user                      | Supports multiple users                                                        |
| Allows access to one entity                     | Allows access to multiple entities                                             |
| Ideal for individual users or small-scale usage | Suitable for organizations or teams working across different data environments |

This flexible licensing model ensures that SCIKIQ can support both individual users and enterprise-level operations efficiently.

***

**Procedure**

As part of registering to SCIK<mark style="color:blue;">**IQ**</mark>, you need to access the **Register Merchant page** using the URL provided, and then input your **name, email Id**, and **merchant type**.&#x20;

Once submitted, an activation email will be sent to your registered email address. To complete the registration, click the activation link in the **email,** enter the **OTP** provided, and **reset your password** to activate your account successfully.

The sections below provide detailed instructions for registering as a merchant in SCIKIQ.

<details>

<summary><strong>Create an Account</strong></summary>

1. Open a **Web Browser**, and then in the **Address Bar**, enter [https://qa4.scikiq.com/merchant/](https://qa4.scikiq.com/merchant/).

The **Register Merchant** page is displayed.

2. In the **Register Merchant** page:

* In the **Name** field, enter your **name**.
* In the **Email** field, enter your **email Id**.
* From the **Merchant Type** drop-down box, select either **Individual License** or **Merchant License** depending on your requirement.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (136).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Registering as a Merchant </p></figcaption></figure>

The success message **Please check your email for login details** is displayed.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Activate Your Account and Reset Password</strong></summary>

1. Open your **Email**, and then click the **Activation Link**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (137).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Activation Link</p></figcaption></figure>

Your account has been activated and you will receive an email containing the OTP and the Reset login URL.

2. Click the **Reset login URL**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (138).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>OTP and Reset URL</p></figcaption></figure>

The Reset your password page is displayed.

2. On the **Reset your password** page:

* Enter the **OTP**.
* Specify a **Password.**
* Confirm **Password.**
* Click the **Submit** button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (139).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Resetting Password</p></figcaption></figure>

The Login to your account page is displayed.

</details>

{% tab title="Recovering Password" %}
**Overview**

This page explains how to recover the forgotten password. The **Forgot Password** functionality in SCIKIQ allows you to recover access to your SCIKIQ account. This feature helps ensure uninterrupted access while maintaining account security.&#x20;

When you select the **Forgot Password** option on the login page, you will be prompted to enter your registered email address. An email containing a one-time password (OTP) will be sent to your registered email ID. Using this verification, you can securely reset your password and regain access to the application.

***

**Procedure**

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to recover the forgotten password.

**Step-by-Step Instructions**

1. On the **Login** page, click **Forgot Password.**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (142).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption><p>Forgot Password Link</p></figcaption></figure>

The Forgot Password page is displayed.

2. On the **Forgot Password** page, enter your registered **Email ID**, and then click the **Submit** button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (143).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption><p>Forgot Password Page</p></figcaption></figure>

The Reset Password page is displayed. Also, an email containing the OTP is sent to your registered email.

2. Open the **Forgot password email,** and then copy the **OTP**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (144).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Code to Reset Password</p></figcaption></figure>

2. Now, on the **Reset Password** page:

* Enter the **OTP**
* Enter the **new** **password.**
* Confirm the **new** **password.**
* Click the **Submit** button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (145).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption><p>Resetting Password</p></figcaption></figure>

The password has been successfully reset, and the Login page is displayed.
