## Overview

This page explains how to recover the forgotten password. The **Forgot Password** functionality in SCIKIQ allows you to recover access to your SCIKIQ account. This feature helps ensure uninterrupted access while maintaining account security.&#x20;

When you select the **Forgot Password** option on the login page, you will be prompted to enter your registered email address. An email containing a one-time password (OTP) will be sent to your registered email ID. Using this verification, you can securely reset your password and regain access to the application.

***

## Procedure

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to recover the forgotten password.

**Step-by-Step Instructions**

1. On the **Login** page, click **Forgot Password.**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (142).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption><p>Forgot Password Link</p></figcaption></figure>

The Forgot Password page is displayed.

2. On the **Forgot Password** page, enter your registered **Email ID**, and then click the **Submit** button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (143).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption><p>Forgot Password Page</p></figcaption></figure>

The Reset Password page is displayed. Also, an email containing the OTP is sent to your registered email.

2. Open the **Forgot password email,** and then copy the **OTP**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (144).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Code to Reset Password</p></figcaption></figure>

2. Now, on the **Reset Password** page:

* Enter the **OTP**
* Enter the **new** **password.**
* Confirm the **new** **password.**
* Click the **Submit** button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/S (145).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption><p>Resetting Password</p></figcaption></figure>

The password has been successfully reset, and the Login page is displayed.
