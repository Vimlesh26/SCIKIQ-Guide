## Overview

This page explains how to log in to the SCIKIQ application. To access the application, open the **SCIKIQ login page**, enter your **username** and **password**, and then click the **Login button**. Upon successful authentication, the [Home Page](/broken/pages/LS7oOsdT5fZXM0xgdrMb) is displayed, allowing you to begin using the application.

***

## Procedure

The below step-by-step instructions help you understand how to login to SCIKIQ.

**Step-by-Step Instructions**

1. Open a **Web Browser**, and then in the **Address Bar**, enter the provided **URL** of SCIKIQ.

The **Login** page is displayed.

2. On the **Login** page, your **user name** and **password.**
3. Click the **Login** button.

**Tip:** Alternatively, you can use the [<mark style="color:blue;">**SSO**</mark>](#user-content-fn-1)[^1] to login to **SCIKIQ** by clicking the **Login with Microsoft** button.
